Focus Mode: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Focus Mode is made by Akash Kush, an independent developer ("we"). It helps students stay focused by blocking distracting apps, short videos and websites they choose. This policy explains what the app handles and why.

What stays on your phone

Focus Mode uses Android's Accessibility service only for the apps you choose to restrict (plus your home screen, to know when you leave them). Other apps, such as banking, payment or chat apps, are never observed. For the apps you chose, it notices when they open and reads only what it needs: the on-screen elements that identify short-video players (such as Reels or Shorts), the website address in your browser's address bar, and the channel name on a YouTube video page. It uses Usage Access to count how long you use apps, for daily limits and your Insights.

This information is processed only on your phone, only to block what you asked it to block and to show you your own statistics. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, never sold, and never used for advertising. Your block lists, schedules, limits, focus sessions and statistics are stored only on your phone and are deleted if you uninstall the app. We have no account system and no servers that hold your data.

Advertising

Focus Mode is free and shows ads from Google AdMob on some screens (never on the block screen or during a focus session). AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location from your IP address, and information about ad interactions to show, measure and limit ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), we ask for your consent first using Google's consent tool, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

App diagnostics

We use Google Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics to fix crashes and understand which features are used. These receive crash reports, device model, Android version, app version and anonymous usage events (for example "a focus session started"). They never include the names of other apps you use, website addresses, YouTube channels, or anything shown on your screen. We use Firebase Remote Config to adjust app settings (such as how often ads appear) without an update.

Permissions

  • Accessibility: to detect and block the apps, short videos, websites and videos you chose (see above). You turn it on yourself after an in-app explanation.
  • Usage Access: to count app time for limits and Insights.
  • Notifications: for the session timer, reminders and alerts if blocking stops working.
  • Alarms & reminders (optional): to make Pomodoro alerts arrive on time.
  • Foreground service: to keep the focus timer running.

Children

Focus Mode is meant for students preparing for exams and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can turn off any blocking feature, revoke any permission in Android Settings, change ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy choices, or uninstall the app to delete all its data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
